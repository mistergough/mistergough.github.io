For the past few years I've been working on my filmmaking skills. I've taken a couple of courses, both focused on documentary, and I've been doing small projects. I'm partly interested in filmmaking for its own sake, but I'm also exploring how it might form a bigger part of my design and facilitation work. I'd love to chat more about this so get in touch if you have an idea for a project we could collaborate on.
